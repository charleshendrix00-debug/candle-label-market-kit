# Candle Label Market Kit

Original editable SVG label templates for small-batch candle makers, market vendors, and home-fragrance brands.

## Product contents

- 8 editable front-label SVG templates
- 1 editable candle care-card SVG template
- A buyer quick-start guide and commercial-use license

## Availability

This public repository documents the product and its terms. It does **not** include the editable template files or a downloadable customer bundle. Those files remain private until they are delivered through a paid sales channel.

## Buyer note

Before using a purchased template on a finished product, replace all sample text, test-print it, and confirm the wording and labelling requirements that apply to the product and location.

No physical product ships. These templates are not legal, safety, testing, or regulatory advice.

## License summary

The customer license permits use of the templates to create labels, packaging inserts, and marketing materials for the buyer's own physical candle or home-fragrance products, including products they sell. It prohibits resale, redistribution, sharing, sublicensing, or repackaging of the editable template files.

No third-party trademarks, brands, or copyrighted artwork are included.
