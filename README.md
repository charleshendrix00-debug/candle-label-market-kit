# Candle Label Market Kit

Original editable SVG label templates for small-batch candle makers, market vendors, and home-fragrance brands.

## What is included

- 8 editable front-label SVG templates
- 1 editable candle care-card SVG template
- A buyer quick-start guide and commercial-use license

## Buyer note

This is a digital template product. The public repository is a preview of the product materials; the customer download must contain only the templates, buyer README, and license.

Before using a template on a finished product, replace all sample text, test-print it, and confirm the wording and labelling requirements that apply to the product and location.

No physical product ships. These templates are not legal, safety, testing, or regulatory advice.
